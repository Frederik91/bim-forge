WASD to walk · drag to look

Move through the model

Walk: focus the model, then use WASD or arrow keys. Drag to look, or enable Mouse look for game-style turning. Q/E also turns. Escape releases the cursor. In Mouse look, click the aimed object to select it.

Fly: WASD follows your facing direction. Space rises, Ctrl descends, and Shift moves faster. Flight can leave the room and pass through geometry. Switch Fly off to return to grounded walking. Frame room returns you to the room or active task.

Plan, Section and Overview retain the task and your Inside pose. Movement stops when you type, leave the model, or switch views.

Free text receives canned simulated replies. The width example is a separate demonstration. Recipient and audience controls are local labels, without message delivery or privacy enforcement.
